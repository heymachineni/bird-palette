# Bird Palette Wiki

Welcome to the **Bird Palette** wiki — a searchable catalog of **bird plumage colors** and **color combinations from nature**.

## Links

- **Live site:** https://birdpalette.web.app
- **GitHub:** https://github.com/heymachineni/bird-palette
- **About the project:** [About](About)
- **SEO strategy:** [SEO](SEO)

## What you can do

- Browse **10,000+ bird color palettes**
- Search by bird name, scientific name, or **hex color**
- Copy plumage swatches for design inspiration
- Explore related color combinations between species
